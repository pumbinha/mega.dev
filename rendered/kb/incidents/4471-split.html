How to tell them apart

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first and the report came by app, email, or a booking-v2 form. The shared parser reads the date month first, so day and month swap.
  • hint: the tenant writes the day first and the report came through the booking-v3 form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Same swap, different code.
  • shift: the date is one day off, not swapped ("Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday"). A month-first parser cannot do that; a time zone can. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but #4471 counted them under the parser by tenant.

The subject line tells none of this: the same subjects appear under all three. That is why merging on subject (decision 0001) put them in one ticket. The tenant, the channel and the body tell them apart. Intake's tag does not: it is a guess from the browser.
